# C8.4 - Gas Stoichiometry

## Important Equations

$$
\begin{gather*}
\frac VT = a \\
\frac{V_1}{T_1} = \frac{V_2}{T_2} \\ \\
PV = k \\
P_1V_1 = P_2V_2 \\ \\
PT = k_\text{const} \\
\frac{P_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2}{T_2} \\ \\
\frac{PV}T = k \\
\frac{P_1V_1}{T_1} = \frac{P_2V_2}{T_2} \\ \\
\frac V n = k \\
\frac{V_1}{n_1} = \frac{V_2}{n_2} \\ \\
PV = nRT \\ \\
n = \frac m M \\
m = nM \\ \\
c = \frac n V \\
n = cV \\
V= \frac n C
\end{gather*}
$$

## Volume-to-Volume Stoichiometry (Same Temp. and Pressure)

**Sample:** A catalytic converter in the exhaust system of a car uses oxygen (from the air) and a catalyst to convert carbon monoxide to carbon dioxide. If the temperature and
pressure remain the same, what volume of oxygen is required to react with 65.0 L of
carbon monoxide produced during a road trip?

1. Write balanced chemical equation

$$
\begin{equation*}
\ce{2CO($g$) + O2($g$) -> 2CO2($g$)}
\end{equation*}
$$

2. Use a volume ratio (like mole ratio but being used for volume)

$$
\begin{align*}
V_\ce{O2} &= V_\ce{CO} \cdot \frac{\ce{1 L O2}}{\ce{2 L CO}} \\
&= \ce{65.0 \cancel{\ce{L CO}}} \cdot \frac{\ce{1 L O2}}{\ce{2 \cancel{\ce{L CO}}}} \\
&= \ce{32.5  L O2}
\end{align*}
$$

3. State final answer

	$\therefore$ 32.5 L of oxygen is required.

## Mass-to-Volume Stoichiometry

**Sample:** What volume of carbon dioxide is produced when 6.40 g of methane gas, $\ce{CH4(g)}$, reacts with excess oxygen? All gases are at 35.0 °C and 100.0 kPa.

1. Convert temp. to Kelvin, if necessary

$$
\begin{align*}
T_K = 35.0 + 273.15 = 308.15\:\text K
\end{align*}
$$

2. Write balanced chemical equation

$$
\begin{equation*}
\ce{CH4($g$) + 2O2($g$) -> CO2($g$) + 2H2O($g$)}
\end{equation*}
$$

3. Convert mass to moles

$$
M_\ce{CH4} = \ce{12.011 g/mol + 4(1.008 g/mol) = 16.043 g/mol}
$$

$$
\begin{align*}
n_\ce{CH4} &= \frac{m_\ce{CH4}}{M_\ce{CH4}} \\
&= \frac{6.40\:\text g}{\ce{16.043 g/mol}} \\
&= \ce{0.398928 mol}
\end{align*}
$$

4. Determine amount of required substance produced

$$
\begin{equation*}
n_\ce{CO2} = \ce{0.398928 \cancel{\ce{mol CH4}}} \cdot \frac{\ce{1 mol CO2}}{\cancel{\ce{1 mol CH4}}} = \ce{0.398928 mol CO2}
\end{equation*}
$$

5. Convert moles of carbon dioxide into volume

$$
\begin{align*}
PV &= nRT \\
V &= \frac{nRT}P \\
V_\ce{CO2} &= \frac{(\ce{0.398928 \cancel{\ce{mol}}})(\ce{8.314 \cancel{\text{kPa}} . L . \cancel{\ce{mol^{-1}}} . \cancel{\ce{K^{-1}}}})(308.15\:\cancel{\text K})}{\ce{100.0 \cancel{\text{kPa}}}} \\
&= 10.2\:\text L
\end{align*}
$$

6. State final answer
 
	$\therefore$ 10.2 L of carbon dioxide is produced.

## Volume-to-Mass Stoichiometry

**Sample:** What mass of sodium azide is required to produce the 67.0 L of nitrogen gas that is required to fill a safety airbag in an automobile? Assume that the gas is produced at a temperature of 32.0 °C and a pressure of 105 kPa.

1. Convert temp. to Kelvin, if necessary

$$
\begin{equation*}
T_K = 32.0 + 273.15 = 305.15\:\text K
\end{equation*}
$$

2. Write balanced chemical equation

$$
\begin{equation*}
\ce{2NaN3($s$) -> 2Na($s$) + 3N2($g$)}
\end{equation*}
$$

3. Convert volume to moles

$$
\begin{align*}
PV &= nRT \\
n &= \frac{PV}{RT} \\
n_\ce{N2} &= \frac{(105\:\cancel{\text{kPa}})(67.0\:\cancel{\text L})}{(\ce{8.314 \cancel{\text{kPa}} . \cancel{\text{L}} . mol^{-1} . \cancel{\ce{K^{-1}}}})(305.15\:\cancel{\text K})} \\
&= 2.772941\text{ mol}
\end{align*}
$$

4. Determine amount of required substance

$$
\begin{equation*}
n_\ce{NaN3} = 2.772941 \cancel{\ce{mol N2}} \cdot \frac{\ce{2 mol NaN3}}{3 \cancel{\ce{mol N2}}} = \ce{1.848627 mol NaN3}
\end{equation*}
$$

5. Convert moles to mass

$$
\begin{gather*}
M_\ce{NaN3} = \ce{22.990 g/mol + 3(14.007 g/mol) = 65.011 g/mol} \\
m_\ce{NaN3} = (1.848627 \cancel{\text{mol}})(65.011\:\text g/\cancel{\text{mol}}) = 12\overline 0\:\text g
\end{gather*}
$$

6. Final statement

	$\therefore$ 120 g of sodium azide is required.
