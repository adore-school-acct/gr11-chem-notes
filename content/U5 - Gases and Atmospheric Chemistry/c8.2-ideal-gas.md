# C8.2 - Ideal Gas

## What is Ideal Gas?

- **ideal gas:** hypothetical gas of particles w/ these properties:
  - entities w/ high translational energy, moving randomly in all dirs. in *straight lines*
  - entity collisons have no loss in kinetic energy (perfectly elastic)
  - volume of ideal gas entity is insignificant (zero) compared to container volume
  - no attractive / repulsive forces
  - do not condense into liquids when cooled
- Ideal gases do not exist
- Serves as a base model to compare or predict gases

## Ideal Gas Law

Uses Charles's Law, Boyle's Law, and Avogadro's Law

- **universal gas constant ($R$):** constant that relates pressure, volume, amount, and temp. of ideal gas
- **ideal gas law:** law stating that product of pressure and volume of gas is directly proportional to amount and abs. temp. of gas

let $V$ be volume of gas, $R$ be universal gas constant, $T$ be abs. temperature (K), $n$ be amount of gas (mol), $P$ be pressure

$$
\begin{gather*}
V = R \cdot \frac{Tn}P \\
PV = nRT
\end{gather*}
$$

If pressure in kPa, vol. in L, and amount of gas in mol, ...

$$
\begin{equation*}
R = 8.314 \:\frac{\text{kPa}\cdot\text L}{\text{mol}\cdot\text K}
\end{equation*}
$$

Molar volume of ideal gas at STP is 22.4 L, derived from Ideal Gas Law
