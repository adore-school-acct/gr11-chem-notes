# C6.4 - Ionic Equations

## Ionic Equations

In solutions where ionic compounds are a solute, ionic compounds dissociate into their ions

i.e. $\text{NaCl}(s)\leftrightharpoons\text{Na}^+(aq)+\text{Cl}^-(aq)$

- **total ionic equations (TIE):** chemical equations that show all dissociated ions of dissolved ionic compounds
- **net ionic equations (NIE):** chemical equations that only show the ions involved in the chemical reaction
- **spectator ions:** ions that do not take part in the reaction

They are typically written for displacement and neutralization reactions

## How to write ionic equations

1. Rewrite dissolved ionic compounds into their individual ions, include state symbol (aq)
   1. when rewriting, make sure to include coefficients if there is more than one ion in compound
2. Leave everything else, like solids, gases, and molecular compounds intact
   1. this includes ionic compounds that aren't dissolved and precipates
3. Simplify if necessary, this is the ***total ionic equation***

### Write Net Ionic Equations

4. Cancel out the ions that haven't changed after the reaction
   1. these are the spectator ions
5. Simplify net ionic equation if necessary

*VERIFY (TIE & NIE):* # of reactants = # of products

### Example

$$
\begin{gather*}
3\text K_2\text{CO}_3(aq) + 2\text{Al}(\text{NO}_3)_3(aq) \rightarrow 6\text{KNO}_3(aq) + \text{Al}_2(\text{CO}_3)_3(s) \\
\textbf{TIE} \\
6\text K^+{(aq)} + 3{\text{CO}_3}^{2-}{(aq)} + 2\text{Al}^{3+}{(aq)} + 6{\text{NO}_3}^-{(aq)} \rightarrow 6\text K^+{(aq)} + 6{\text{NO}_3}^-{(aq)} + \text{Al}_2(\text{CO}_3)_3(s) \\
\textbf{NIE} \\
2\text{Al}^{3+}{(aq)} + 3{\text{CO}_3}^{2-}(aq) \rightarrow \text{Al}_2(\text{CO}_3)_3(s)
\end{gather*}
$$
