# C3.2 - Synthesis and Decompositon Reactions

## Synthesis

**synthesis reaction:** chemical reaction where 2+ substances combine to form a larger product

***General Equation***

$$
\begin{equation*}
A+B\rightarrow AB
\end{equation*}
$$

### General Types of Synthesis Reactions

1. 2 elements &rarr; binary compound
2. element + compound &rarr; new compound
3. 2 compounds &rarr; new compound

### Types in Detail

*Due to $\LaTeX$ difficulties, heat/electricity is not written on top of arrow*
- (1) metal + non-metal &rarr; binary ionic compound
  - i.e. $3\text{Ca}(s)+\text{N}_2(g)\rightarrow\text{Ca}_3\text N_2(s)$
- (1) non-metal + non-metal &rarr; binary covalent compound
  - i.e. $\text C(s)+\text O_2(g)\rightarrow\text{CO}_2(g)$
- (2) non-metal + covalent compound &rarr; compound with combined atoms of reactants
  - i.e. $\text{PCl}_3(l)+\text{Cl}_2(g)\rightarrow\text{PCl}_5(l)$
- (3) metal oxide + water &rarr; metal hydroxide (base)
  - i.e. $\text K_2\text O(s)+\text H_2\text O(l)\rightarrow2\text{KOH}(aq)$
- (3) non-metal oxide + water &rarr; oxyacid
  - oxyacid = atoms of non-metal oxide + 1 oxygen with ionic bond to hydrogen
  - i.e. $\text{CO}_2(g)+\text H_2\text O(l)\rightarrow\text H_2\text{CO}_3(aq)$
- (3) metal oxide + carbon dioxide &rarr; metal carbonate
  - i.e. $\text{HgO}(s)+\text{CO}_2(g)\rightarrow\text{Hg}_2\text{CO}_3(s)$

## Decomposition

**decomposition:** reaction where large/complex compound breaks down into 2+ simpler products

- opposite of synthesis
- recognizable with only substance on left side of arrow
- usually needs energy to start (i.e. heat, electricity)
- "heat" or "electricity" is usually written above the arrow

***General Equation***

$$
\newcommand\e{\mathrel{\stackrel{\text{energy}}{\rightarrow}}}
\begin{equation*}
AB\e A + B
\end{equation*}
$$

### Types of Decomposition

- binary compound &rarr; element 1 + element 2
  - i.e. $2\text{HgO}\rightarrow2\text{Hg}+\text O_2$
- metal carbonate &rarr; metal oxide + carbon dioxide
  - i.e. $\text{Na}_2\text{CO}_3\rightarrow\text{Na}_2\text O+\text{CO}_2$
- metal chlorate &rarr; binary salt + oxygen
  - i.e. $\text{Ba}(\text{ClO}_3)_2\rightarrow\text{BaCl}_2+3\text O_2$
- metal hydroxide (base) &rarr; metal oxide + water
  - i.e. $2\text{NaOH}\rightarrow\text{Na}_2\text O+\text H_2\text O$
- oxyacid &rarr; non-metal oxide + water
  - i.e. $\text H_2\text{CO}_3\rightarrow\text{CO}_2+\text H_2\text O$
