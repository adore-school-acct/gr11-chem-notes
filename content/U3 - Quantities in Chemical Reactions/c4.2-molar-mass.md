# C4.2 - Molar Mass

## Molar Mass

**molar mass:** mass of 1 mole of a substance

- unit: g/mol
- symbol: $M$
- symbol for water: $M_{\text H_2 \text O}$

## Molar Mass of Elements

The molar mass of an element has the same numerical value as the element's atomic mass

$$
\begin{equation*}
\frac M {\text{g/mol}} = \frac A {\text u}
\end{equation*}
$$

i.e. If iron has atomic mass of 55.85 u, then iron's molar mass is 55.85 g/mol

## Molar Mass of Compounds

Equivalent to the sum of the molar masses of all atoms that compose the compound

*General Equation* ($\text A_x \text B_y$)

$$
\begin{equation*}
M_{\text A_x \text B_y} = xM_\text A + yM_\text B
\end{equation*}
$$

*General Equation for Hydrate* ($\text A_x \text B_y \cdot z \text H_2 \text O$)

$$
\begin{equation*}
M_{\text A_x \text B_y \cdot z \text H_2 \text O} = xM_\text A + yM_\text B + zM_{\text H_2 \text O}
\end{equation*}
$$

*Example of calcium phosphate, equation only*

Formula: $\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2$

$$
\begin{equation*}
M_{\text{Ca}_3(\text{PO}_4)_2} = 3M_\text{Ca} + 2(M_\text P + 4M_\text O)
\end{equation*}
$$

## Conversion between Mass and Mole

let $m$ be mass (g), $M$ be molar mass, $n$ be amount of substance (mol)

$$
\begin{gather*}
n = \frac m M \\
m = M n
\end{gather*}
$$

## Conversion between Mass and # of Entities

**Convert from Mass to Number of Entities:**

1. Find molar mass of entity
2. Convert from mass to moles using molar mass
3. Convert from moles to entities

**Convert from Number of Entities to Mass:**

1. Find molar mass of entity
2. Convert from entities to moles
3. Convert from moles to mass using molar mass

*Direct equations for steps 2-3*

$$
\begin{gather*}
m = M \frac N {N_A} \tag{ent to mass} \\
N = \frac m M N_A \tag{mass to ent}
\end{gather*}
$$
