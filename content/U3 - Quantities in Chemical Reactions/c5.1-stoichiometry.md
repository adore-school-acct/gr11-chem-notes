# C5.1 - Stoichiometry

## What is Stoichiometry?

**stoichiometry:** calculation of amounts of materials from known quantities/ratios in the reaction

*Balanced chemical equation is required*

**stoichiometric coefficient:** coefficient of reactants and products in a chemical reaction

## Mole Ratio

**mole ratio:** ratio between amounts of two substances

$$
\begin{equation*}
\text{mole ratio} = \frac{\text{moles of \textbf{required} substance}}{\text{moles of \textbf{given} substance}}
\end{equation*}
$$

- moles of substances determined by coefficients in a balanced equation
- good to simplify ratio to smallest whole number possible on top and bottom

## Determined Required Moles from Mole Ratio

let $n_R$ be mole ratio, $n_r$ be amount of required sub. (mol), $n_g$ be amount of given sub. (mol)

$$
\begin{gather*}
n_R = \frac{n_{Rr}}{n_{Rg}} \\
n_r = n_g n_R = n_g \frac{n_{Rr}}{n_{Rg}}
\end{gather*}
$$

i.e. If there is 3.00 mol of $\text{K}$, must find $\text K_2 \text O$ in reaction below:

$$
\begin{equation*}
2\text K + \text{CuO} \rightarrow \text K_2 \text O + \text{Cu}
\end{equation*}
$$

$$
\begin{gather*}
n_R = \frac{1 \text{ mol K}_2 \text O}{2 \text{ mol K}} \\
n_r = (3.00 \cancel{\text{ mol K}})(\frac{1 \text{ mol K}_2 \text O}{2 \cancel{\text{ mol K}}}) = 1.50 \text{ mol K}_2 \text O
\end{gather*}
$$
