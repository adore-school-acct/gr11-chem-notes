# C4.1 - Moles

## The Mole

**mole:** measures amount of entities in a given substance

- unit symbol: mol
- $1 \text{ mol} \approx 6.02 \times 10^{23} \text{ entities}$
- mathematical symbol: $n$
- equal to exactly the number of atoms in 12 g of carbon-12

Used since there are way too many small particles in a given substance

### Avogrado's Constant

**Avogrado constant ($N_A$):** $6.02214076 \times 10^{23} \: (\text{entities})$

$N_A$ rounds to $6.02 \times 10^{23}$

## Entities

**entity** *(a.k.a. representative particle)*: the base unit of a substance

Entities change depending on the type of substance:

|Substance|Entities|
|-|-|
|Element|atoms|
|Molecular compound|molecules|
|Ionic compound|formula units|

$N$ is the variable used for number of entities

## Conversion between Entities and Moles

let $N$ be number of entities, $n$ be amount of substance (mol), $N_A$ be Avogrado's constant

$$
\begin{gather*}
N = n N_A \tag{mol to ent} \\
n = \frac N {N_A} \tag{ent to mol} \\
\end{gather*}
$$

- Unit for $N$: atoms, molecules, or formula units (entities)
- Unit for $n$: mol

## Equation Subscripting

Subscript of variable chemical formula for element or compound

*Example of subscripts for water:*

$$
\begin{gather*}
N \rightarrow N_{\text H_2 \text O} \\
n \rightarrow n_{\text H_2 \text O}
\end{gather*}
$$

Will also be used for molar mass
