# C4.3 - Percentage Composition

## Percentage Compositon

**percentage composition:** % of an element in a compound or substance

math symbol: $p$ *(unspecified, made up by me)*

## Percentage Compostion via Mass

let $p$ be percentage compostion, let $E$ be unknown element, let $m_E$ be mass of E, let $m_\text{comp}$ be mass of compound

$$
\begin{equation*}
p_E=\frac{m_E}{m_\text{comp}}\cdot100\%
\end{equation*}
$$

## Percentage Composition of Compound via Formula

use above variables, let $a$ be # of atoms of element / entity, let $M$ be molar mass

$$
\begin{equation*}
p_E = \frac{a_E M_E}{M_\text{comp}} \cdot 100\%
\end{equation*}
$$
